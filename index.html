SafeHonolulu

safehonolulu.com

A neighborhood safety map for Honolulu County. See and share reports about road hazards, flooding, outages, beach conditions, and severe weather.

  • Live traffic, satellite view, and quick jumps to every part of Oʻahu
  • Report an incident by typing or by voice
  • Free for the Oʻahu community
Open SafeHonolulu ↗

Community reports may be unverified. SafeHonolulu is not monitored by emergency services. For emergencies, call 911.

Ask Hawaiʻi

askaihawaii.com

Checked answers about Hawaiʻi for visitors and locals. Reservations, hikes, beaches and ocean safety, local laws, culture, food, and events, each with its source and date.

  • Ask by typing or by voice, island by island
  • Live National Weather Service alerts and forecasts
  • Works inside AI assistants like Claude as a connector
